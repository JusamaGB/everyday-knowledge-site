EVERYDAY KNOWLEDGE

Short quizzes. Real sources.

Everyday Knowledge is a YouTube Shorts channel of quick quizzes on capitals, currencies, authors, elements, paintings and more. Every answer is checked against its source, and the source is linked under each video.

Watch: youtube.com/@everydayknowledgequiz

How it works: questions and facts come from Wikidata (CC0) and Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0); nothing is invented. Comment your score, and vote on the next theme in the comments.

About the Quiz Shorts app

Quiz Shorts is the name of the private tool that makes and publishes the channel's videos through Google's YouTube APIs. It is used only by the channel's operator, on the operator's own computer, and only on this channel. What it accesses and how data is handled is set out in the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
